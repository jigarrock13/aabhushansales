About Us

A Legacy of Exquisite
Craftsmanship

At Aabhushan Sales, we have been shaping the world of micro imitation jewellery for over two decades. Based in the jewellery capital of India, Surat, our workshop blends traditional artistry with modern precision engineering.

Every piece we create undergoes rigorous quality checks — from raw material selection to the final polish. We serve retailers, wholesalers, and exporters across India and beyond, delivering jewellery that looks luxurious without compromise.
